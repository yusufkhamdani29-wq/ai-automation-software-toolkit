# Weekly Review (15 minutes, every Friday)

1. **Tasks:** What got done? What rolls over, and why?
2. **Money:** Which invoices are still open? Send reminders for anything 7+ days overdue.
3. **Clients:** Any client waiting on you? Reply today.
4. **Next week:** Pick the top 3 goals. Put them on the home page.
5. **One improvement:** What is one small process you will automate or simplify next week?
