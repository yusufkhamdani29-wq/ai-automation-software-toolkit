# My First Product Launch: 3 Small Fixes That Doubled My Replies

I sell digital photo packs. Last month I launched a new pack and got 4
replies from 60 emails I sent. This month I changed three small things
and got 9 replies from 60 emails. Nothing magic — just clearer writing
and one follow-up.

## Fix 1: One idea per email

Before, my launch email tried to do everything: announce the pack, list
every photo, explain the licence, and ask for a share. Nobody could tell
what I wanted them to do. Now each email has one job. The launch email
announces. The second email answers questions. The third email is the
reminder.

## Fix 2: The subject line says what is inside

"New pack!!!" got ignored. "20 new food photos — $2 this week" got
opened, because the reader could decide in two seconds whether it was
for them. No hype words, no fake urgency, just the facts.

## Fix 3: One follow-up, once

I used to send the launch email and never follow up. Now I send one
short follow-up three days later to people who did not open the first
one: "In case you missed it — 20 food photos, $2, here is the link."
Half of this month's replies came from that one email.
