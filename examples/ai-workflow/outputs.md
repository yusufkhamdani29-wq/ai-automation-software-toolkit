# Worked Example 3 — AI Workflow: Content Repurposing end to end

## What this shows

One short blog post (`input_blog_post.md`) turned into 5 social posts, a
newsletter section, and a 60-second video script, using Workflow 01
(Content Repurposing) from `prompts/01-content-repurposing.md`.

The outputs below are the finished drafts from that workflow. They were
written by applying the workflow's prompts to the input post, then
reviewed once: names and numbers were checked against the original
(4 replies from 60 emails last month, 9 from 60 this month — both kept
exactly as written).

## The 5 social posts

**Post 1 — the result**
> I sent 60 launch emails and got 4 replies. This month I changed 3 small things and got 9. Here is what changed:

**Post 2 — one idea per email**
> My old launch email tried to do everything at once: announce, list, explain, ask. Nobody knew what to do. Now each email has one job. What is the one job of your last email?

**Post 3 — the subject line**
> "New pack!!!" got ignored. "20 new food photos — $2 this week" got opened. The reader could decide in 2 seconds. What does your subject line promise?

**Post 4 — the follow-up**
> I used to send one launch email and never follow up. One short follow-up 3 days later brought half of this month's replies. Do you follow up, or send once and hope?

**Post 5 — the summary**
> 3 small fixes, 4 replies to 9: one idea per email, a subject line that says what is inside, and one follow-up. Which one are you skipping?

## Newsletter section (118 words)

> Last month I sent 60 launch emails and got 4 replies. This month I
> changed three small things and got 9. First, one idea per email — the
> launch email announces, nothing else. Second, a subject line that says
> what is inside: "20 new food photos — $2 this week" beats "New
> pack!!!". Third, one follow-up three days later for people who did not
> open the first email; half of this month's replies came from that
> single email. Nothing magic — clearer writing and one follow-up.
> Read the full post.

## 60-second video script

> I sent 60 launch emails and got 4 replies. Then I changed three small
> things, and got 9. First: one idea per email. Second: a subject line
> that says what is inside. Third: one follow-up, three days later.
> That follow-up brought half the replies. Try one of the three this
> week — and tell me which one you picked.

## How long it took

Writing the input post took about 20 minutes. Running the three prompts
and reviewing the outputs took about 10 minutes. Writing all eight
pieces from scratch would have taken over an hour.
