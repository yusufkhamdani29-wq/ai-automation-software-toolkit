# Workflow 02 — Email Triage — Clear the Inbox in 20 Minutes

## The problem

The inbox mixes urgent client mail, newsletters, invoices, and spam. You re-read the same emails three times before answering any of them.

## What you need

Your email list (or 5–10 copied emails) and an AI chat tool. No inbox access is given to the AI — you paste the text yourself.

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Sort the inbox

```
Here are the emails I need to triage:
[PASTE 5-10 EMAILS, SUBJECT + FIRST LINES]

Sort them into four groups: 1) Reply today, 2) Reply this week, 3) File / reference, 4) Delete or unsubscribe. For each email give one line: why it belongs in that group.
```

### Prompt 2: Draft the replies

```
Draft short replies for the 'Reply today' group. Each reply: under 80 words, polite, direct, and ending with a clear next step or question. Use a placeholder [NAME] where you are unsure of the recipient's name. Do not promise dates or prices I have not given you.
```

## Steps

1. Copy the subject lines and first lines of unread emails into prompt 1.
2. Work through the four groups: delete group 4 first, file group 3, then answer group 1 with the drafts from prompt 2.
3. Set a 20-minute timer. When it rings, stop — the rest waits until tomorrow's triage.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool. Your normal email app. Optional: email filters/rules to auto-file newsletters before they reach the inbox.

## Time saved

Manual triage of 30 unread emails often takes 45+ minutes of re-reading. This workflow usually takes 15–20 minutes, most of it spent on the actual replies.
