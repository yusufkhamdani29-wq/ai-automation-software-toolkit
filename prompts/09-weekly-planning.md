# Workflow 09 — Weekly Planning — Plan the Week in 15 Minutes

## The problem

Monday starts with the inbox, and by Wednesday the important work has not started. The week happens to you instead of the other way round.

## What you need

Your top 3 goals for the week, your fixed appointments, and an AI chat tool.

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Build the week

```
My top 3 goals this week are:
1. [GOAL]
2. [GOAL]
3. [GOAL]

My fixed commitments are: [APPOINTMENTS]. I have about [X] focused hours per day.

Build a weekday plan. Each day gets: one main task (tied to a goal), one smaller task, and one thing to ignore until next week. Keep it realistic — do not schedule more than the hours I gave. Present it as a simple table.
```

### Prompt 2: Write the daily check-in

```
Write a 5-line daily check-in I can paste into my notes each morning: today's main task, the one thing that would make today a win, any blocker, and the first 10-minute action.
```

## Steps

1. Run prompt 1 on Monday (or Sunday evening). Read the plan and cross out anything unrealistic before the week starts.
2. Each morning, fill in the check-in from prompt 2. It takes 2 minutes and sets the day's priority.
3. On Friday, spend 5 minutes noting what finished and what rolls over — that becomes next week's input.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool. A calendar and a notes app.

## Time saved

Planning is 15 minutes that typically saves an hour of mid-week re-prioritising and context-switching. The saving is in focus, not just minutes.
