# Workflow 05 — Social Scheduling — Plan a Week of Posts from One Theme

## The problem

You open the app to post and stare at a blank box. Posting daily feels impossible when every post starts from zero.

## What you need

One weekly theme (e.g. 'pricing mistakes' or 'behind the scenes') and an AI chat tool.

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Plan the week

```
My theme this week is: [THEME]. My audience is: [AUDIENCE]. My product is: [PRODUCT, one line].

Plan 5 posts, one per weekday. For each post give: the day, a one-line hook, the main point in 2 sentences, and the format (tip, story, question, example, or offer). Exactly one of the five posts may mention my product. No hashtags.
```

### Prompt 2: Draft the posts

```
Write out posts 1, 3, and 5 in full, each under 280 characters. Keep the hooks from the plan. Plain language, no hype words like 'amazing' or 'game-changing'.
```

## Steps

1. Pick the theme on Monday morning (or Sunday evening) and run prompt 1.
2. Draft the posts with prompt 2, then read them aloud — if a sentence sounds like an advert, rewrite it.
3. Schedule all five at once so the week runs itself.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool. A free scheduler (Buffer free plan, Meta Business Suite, or each platform's native scheduler).

## Time saved

Writing 5 posts from scratch across a week typically costs 60–90 minutes of starting and stopping. Planning them in one 20-minute session usually cuts that to about half.
