# Workflow 01 — Content Repurposing — One Idea, Five Posts

## The problem

You wrote one good blog post or recorded one video, and then it sits there. Turning it into social posts, a newsletter section, and a short script takes another hour you do not have.

## What you need

Your original text (blog post, video transcript, or rough notes) and an AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini free tier).

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Turn one post into five social posts

```
Here is my blog post:
[PASTE YOUR POST]

Turn it into 5 short social media posts. Each post must stand alone, keep one clear idea, use a different hook, stay under 280 characters, and end with a simple question. Do not add hashtags. Do not invent facts that are not in the post.
```

### Prompt 2: Write the newsletter version

```
Here is the same blog post:
[PASTE YOUR POST]

Write a newsletter section about it in 120 words or fewer. Start with the reader's problem, give the single most useful point from the post, and end with one link-style call to action: 'Read the full post'. Keep the tone plain and direct.
```

### Prompt 3: Make a 60-second video script

```
Here is my blog post:
[PASTE YOUR POST]

Write a 60-second video script based on it. Three parts: a 5-second hook that states the problem, three short points in plain language, and a closing line that tells the viewer what to do next. No stage directions, just the spoken words.
```

## Steps

1. Paste your original post into prompt 1 and save the 5 posts.
2. Run prompt 2 for the newsletter version and prompt 3 for the video script.
3. Read every output once. Fix names, numbers, and links — AI can garble them.
4. Schedule the posts across the week instead of publishing all at once.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — free tiers work). A scheduler such as Buffer's free plan or the native scheduler in each platform.

## Time saved

Repurposing by hand typically takes 45–60 minutes. With these prompts, the drafts take about 10–15 minutes plus a 5-minute review. Your mileage depends on how much editing the drafts need.
