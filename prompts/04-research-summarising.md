# Workflow 04 — Research Summarising — Read the Article in 2 Minutes

## The problem

You bookmark long articles, reports, and papers to read 'later' — and later never comes. When you finally open one, the useful part is three paragraphs buried in thirty.

## What you need

The article text (paste it, or paste the link's key sections) and an AI chat tool.

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Summarise it

```
Here is an article I need to understand:
[PASTE TEXT]

Summarise it in this structure: 1) The main point in one sentence. 2) Three key findings, one line each. 3) One thing the author recommends doing. 4) One limitation or thing the article does not cover. Keep the whole summary under 250 words. Do not add information that is not in the article.
```

### Prompt 2: Turn it into actions

```
Based on that summary, list 3 actions I could take this week, each under 15 words. Then list 2 questions the article raises that I should investigate before acting.
```

## Steps

1. Paste the article into prompt 1. Read the summary — if a key finding looks wrong, re-read that part of the original.
2. Run prompt 2 and pick at most one action to do this week.
3. Save the summary with the article link in your notes. Delete the bookmark.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool. A read-later app (Pocket, Instapaper, or your browser bookmarks) for the source list.

## Time saved

Reading a 3,000-word article takes 15–20 minutes; a summary takes about 2 minutes to read and 1 minute to generate. You still read the full article when the decision matters — this is for triage, not replacement.
