# Workflow 08 — Lead Qualification — Score Enquiries Before You Reply

## The problem

Every enquiry feels urgent, so you reply to all of them — including the ones with no budget, no deadline, and a vague brief. Hours disappear into calls that never become work.

## What you need

Your qualification criteria (budget range, project type, timeline) and the enquiry text.

## Copy-paste prompts

### Prompt 1: Score the lead

```
Score this enquiry against my criteria:
- Budget: [YOUR RANGE, e.g. $500+]
- Project type: [WHAT YOU DO]
- Timeline: [e.g. start within 4 weeks]

Enquiry:
[PASTE ENQUIRY]

Give a score out of 10 and one line per criterion: Met / Not met / Unclear. Then recommend one of: Reply today / Reply with a clarifying question / Decline politely. Do not invent details about the enquiry that are not in the text.
```

### Prompt 2: Draft the reply

```
Write the reply for the recommendation above. If it is a clarifying question, ask at most 3 questions (budget, timeline, scope). If it is a decline, thank them, say plainly that the project is not a fit, and wish them well — under 60 words.
```

## Steps

1. Write your criteria down once (budget, type, timeline) and keep them next to your inbox.
2. Score every new enquiry before replying — it takes 2 minutes and stops bad-fit calls.
3. Track the scores in a sheet for a month: if good leads keep scoring low, your criteria (or your marketing) need adjusting.

## Tools

Any AI chat tool. A simple sheet for tracking scores.

## Time saved

A 30-minute discovery call with a bad-fit lead costs 30 minutes plus preparation. Scoring first takes about 2 minutes per enquiry and typically filters out the obvious mismatches before they reach your calendar.
