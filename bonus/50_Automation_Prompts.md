# Bonus — 50 Automation Prompts

Copy any prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your own details, and paste it into your AI chat tool.
These are starting points: always read the output before using it.

## Repurposing

1. Turn this article into a 5-post thread, one idea per post, each under 280 characters.

2. Rewrite this paragraph for a 12-year-old without losing the meaning.

3. Turn these meeting notes into a one-paragraph update for a client.

4. Make a checklist from this how-to article, max 10 items.

5. Turn this product description into three ad headlines, each under 10 words.

## Writing

6. Rewrite this email to be half as long without losing any fact.

7. Make this text more direct: remove filler words and passive voice.

8. Write a 50-word introduction for a blog post about [TOPIC].

9. Give me 5 subject lines for an email about [TOPIC], plain and honest, no clickbait.

10. Turn these bullet points into a short paragraph that flows naturally.

## Email

11. Draft a polite 'no' to a request I cannot take on, under 60 words.

12. Write a thank-you email to a client after a project ends, under 80 words.

13. Draft an out-of-office reply: away until [DATE], urgent matters to [CONTACT].

14. Summarise this email thread in 5 bullets: what was asked, what was decided, what is still open.

15. Rewrite this angry draft email to be firm but professional before I send it.

## Planning

16. Break this goal into 5 steps I can do this week, each under 30 minutes.

17. Given these 6 tasks and 3 hours, order them by impact and tell me what to drop.

18. Make a packing list for a 3-day work trip: laptop, chargers, documents, clothes.

19. Turn this vague goal into a specific one with a number and a deadline.

20. List the 3 biggest risks in this plan and one mitigation for each.

## Research

21. Extract the 5 most important numbers from this report, with one line of context each.

22. Compare these two options in a table: cost, time, risk, effort.

23. What are the common mistakes beginners make with [TOPIC]? List 5 with a one-line fix each.

24. Explain [TOPIC] to me as if I know nothing about it, in 150 words.

25. Find the weak points in this argument: [PASTE ARGUMENT].

## Sales

26. Write a 3-sentence product description for [PRODUCT] that states the benefit first.

27. List 5 objections a buyer might have about [PRODUCT] and a one-line honest answer for each.

28. Write a follow-up message for a prospect who went quiet after a quote, under 60 words.

29. Turn this feature list into benefits: one line per feature, starting with what the buyer gets.

30. Write a simple refund-policy paragraph: fair to buyers, clear about digital products.

## Social

31. Give me 5 post ideas about [TOPIC] that teach something instead of selling.

32. Write a post that asks my audience one good question about [TOPIC].

33. Turn this testimonial into a short post, keeping the customer's exact words for the quote.

34. Write a 'what I learned this week' post in 100 words, honest tone, no hype.

35. Suggest 3 poll questions about [TOPIC] for my audience.

## Productivity

36. Look at my task list and tell me which 3 to do first and why: [PASTE LIST].

37. Turn this messy note into a clean task list with owners and deadlines.

38. Write a 2-minute morning routine for a workday, realistic, no miracle claims.

39. Help me say no to this meeting: draft a 2-line decline with an alternative.

40. Summarise my day from these notes in 4 bullets for tomorrow's handover.

## Finance

41. From this expense list, total by category and flag the largest one: [PASTE LIST].

42. Explain the difference between revenue and profit in 3 sentences with a small example.

43. Draft an invoice reminder: invoice [NUMBER], [AMOUNT], due [DATE], friendly tone.

44. If I earn [AMOUNT] per sale and sell [N] per month, what is my monthly revenue? Show the working.

45. List 5 small costs that quietly eat a freelancer's profit, with a one-line fix each.

## Automation

46. List every step of this manual task, then mark which steps a spreadsheet formula could do: [DESCRIBE TASK].

47. Write a simple checklist for automating a repetitive task: trigger, action, check, fallback.

48. Suggest 3 free tools that could replace this manual process: [DESCRIBE PROCESS].

49. Draft the rules for an email filter: newsletters go to a folder, invoices get starred.

50. What should I automate first? Rank these 5 tasks by time saved vs setup effort: [LIST].
